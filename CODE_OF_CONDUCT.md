# Code of conduct

Delphi Modernizer adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for every repository and community space of the organization.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the
community. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the organization owners through GitHub, or with GitHub's **Report abuse**
link on the content in question. Reports are handled confidentially, and the Covenant's enforcement guidelines decide
the response.
